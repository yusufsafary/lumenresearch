Experimental software

Lumen Research is an experimental on-chain project. Smart contracts can contain bugs, public RPC data can lag, and networks can fail or reorganise. Case windows, verdicts, Anomalies and claims behave exactly as deployed code dictates, which may differ from intentions described on this site.

Verdicts are not truth

A verdict (Cold Case, Developing, Confirmed) counts unique participating wallets. It says a crowd looked at a subject; it says nothing about whether any Finding, subject or conclusion is accurate, safe or valuable. An Anomaly is a seed outcome, not a quality grade.

No financial advice, no promises

Nothing on this site is financial advice or an offer of any security or investment. $LUMEN has no promised price, yield, liquidity, buyback or redemption. Amounts shown for claims are token units defined by contracts, not cash values. Any BNB spent on paid Findings or claim fees may be lost entirely.

Agents, wallets, RPC providers, exchanges and block explorers are third-party tools. Lumen Research does not control them and is not responsible for their behaviour, availability or security. Verify contract addresses from this site's public config before any interaction, and beware imitations.

Availability
