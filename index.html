Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly (name, institutional email, organization), information generated through use of the platform (papers indexed, workspace activity, collaboration logs), and technical data (IP address, browser type, device type) for security and analytics purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to provide and improve the LumenResearch platform, communicate with you about your account and product updates, ensure security and prevent fraud, and generate anonymized aggregate analytics for product development.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data with trusted service providers (hosting, analytics, support) who are contractually bound to protect your information. We may disclose data when required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time via Settings > Data Export, or by contacting privacy@lumenresearch.ltd. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

5. Security

LumenResearch is SOC 2 Type II certified and ISO 27001 compliant. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We conduct annual third-party penetration testing and maintain a responsible disclosure program.

6. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries: privacy@lumenresearch.ltd · LumenResearch Ltd., 100 Main St, Cambridge, MA 02142
