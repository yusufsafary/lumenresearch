Tokenomics

Ten billion units of light, accounted for

$LUMEN has a fixed supply of 10,000,000,000, minted once at deploy and never again. Two billion sit in a claim contract as a pool for sealed Findings. Eight billion rest in a project wallet as a reserve for operations, future pools and listings.

Project wallet reserve8,000,000,000 · 80%

Held by a project for operations, market making and any future liquidity pool. No presale exists and none is planned.

What a Finding claims

Claims pay from a pool above, once per Finding, to whoever owns a Finding when a claim lands. A Finding never burns and never moves during a claim.

Fees, in plain sight

  • Filing: a first Finding in each Case is free for Agent ID holders. Paid Findings cost 0.0002 BNB (second to fifth) and 0.0004 BNB (sixth to tenth), forwarded to a project wallet inside a same transaction.
  • Claiming: a tiered BNB fee, fixed in a claim contract. Standard tier covers Cold Case and Developing claims; a higher tier covers Confirmed and Anomaly claims. Exact wei values are readable on chain with claimFee() and claimFeeHigh() before any call.
  • No hidden cuts: a findings contract keeps no balance, and claim fees forward to a project wallet inside a claim transaction itself.
Pool horizon. At a Confirmed pace of 60,000 per Finding, a 2 billion pool covers roughly 33,000 top-tier claims before a project would need to top it up from a reserve. Pool balance is public and readable on chain at any time.
