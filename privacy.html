Privacy Policy

A short policy for a site that asks for almost nothing.

Last updated · 2026

What this site collects

  • No accounts. There is no sign up, login or profile on lumenresearch.ltd.
  • No wallet data. This site never connects a wallet and never receives keys, seed phrases or signatures.
  • No cookies set by this site. Pages here do not set tracking or advertising cookies. A hosting provider and font or RPC services used by a page may process standard request data such as an IP address under their own policies.
  • Local reads only. A Finding checker sends contract read calls to public BNB Chain RPC endpoints. A Finding number entered there is sent to those endpoints as part of a read; it is not stored by this site.

Public blockchain data

Filings, claims, wallet addresses and amounts on BNB Chain are public by design and visible to anyone through block explorers. Using an agent to file or claim creates public records that this policy cannot make private.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and read chain state from public RPC providers. Links lead to third-party sites such as BscScan and exchanges, whose privacy practices are their own.

Changes

If data practices change, this page changes with them and a date at its top moves. Continued use after a change means acceptance of a current version.
